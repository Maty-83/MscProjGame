	Controls to be implemented:
	
		>Mouse controls:
			>Two ways you can do mouse controls.
				>Direct: 
					>Mouse controls acceleration (Mouse moving = accelerate in that direction)
					>Great if you don't damp the acceleration (Precise frame-by-frame control)
					>By modifying the mouse delta sensitivity, we can achieve extremely precise control in a given axis.
					>Would be good to have a "Control overflow" feature, so exact frame and control timing doesn't influence acceleration, only total displacement does.
						>Counter on mouse.
				>Indirect:
					>Mouse displacement controls maximum (rotational) velocity
					>If you intend to damp inputs, having this continuous input is a better idea.
					>Implementation is also fairly straightforward. We accelerate towards the desired total vector.
					>Disadvantage: No way to "clear" input precisely becomes a significantly greater issue than a direct control.
						>Needs a way to clear these (Already partially implemented): Middle mouse is perfect for this.
					
		>Throttle control:
			>If we're damping inputs, keeping a continuous throttle allows us having a player constantly keep the thrust button pressed.
			>Inherited from airplanes as they are.
			>Many implementations across different genres (As just one example, WT uses it for planes), so would be good to test for our purposes.
			>Precise control again becomes an issue.
				>"Throttle cut" key? E:D uses 'X' for it.
				>Or "Stop and re-press" when reaching zero?
